# Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-08-23

Titan's Glyph Grimoire is a browser-based fan game. This notice explains, in
plain language, what data the app handles. Short version: it collects nothing.

## What is stored on your device

The app saves two settings in your browser's `localStorage` so they survive a
page reload:

| Key                | Purpose                              | Values     |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ---------- |
| `tgg.practiceMode` | Remembers whether trace mode was on  | `'1'`/`'0'`|
| `tgg.muted`        | Remembers whether audio was muted    | `'1'`/`'0'`|

These values never leave your browser. Clearing your site data removes them.

## What we do not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
- No cookies or third-party trackers.
- No advertising.
- No network requests beyond loading the app itself; everything runs
  client-side after load. Drawings, scores, and settings are processed
  entirely on your device.

## GDPR / CCPA

Because no personal data is collected, transmitted, or stored on any server,
there is nothing to access, export, or delete on our side. The only stored
state is the two localStorage keys above, which you control through your
browser.
